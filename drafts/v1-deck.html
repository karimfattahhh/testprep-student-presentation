What is the SAT?

One test, two sections, one score. Universities use it to compare students from very different schools on the same scale.

Why do students take it?

Because it opens doors.

01

University admission

Many universities in the US and around the world use the SAT to compare applicants from different schools and countries.

02

Scholarships

A strong score can strengthen merit scholarship and financial aid applications.

03

A common measure

Your grades come from your school. The SAT puts every applicant on the same scale, so a strong score speaks for you anywhere.

Good to knowRequirements differ by university and by country. Check each one you apply to; that's exactly what WeGuide helps with.
